              Structures

                Spawn & starting positions

                • Each country starts inside a circular territory proportional to the board size. The circle's radius scales so every country gets a fair footprint.
                • 🎲 Random spawn (default): countries are placed at random positions on the board with a minimum distance of max(height, width) ÷ number of countries between any two centers.
                • Fixed spawn (unchecked): countries spawn at corners first (top-left, bottom-right, top-right, bottom-left), then edge midpoints if more than 4 countries.
                • Each country is guaranteed at least one city inside its starting circle — if the map generator didn't place one there, a city is created under the HQ.
                • Starting units are placed inside the spawn circle, clustered near the center. The HQ spawns at the circle's center.
                • Cities and villages inside a country's spawn circle are assigned to that country. Sites outside all circles start neutral.

                Map Seed

                • Every map is generated from a seed number. The same seed + board size + terrain weights always produces the exact same map.
                • You can enter a numeric seed or any text string (it will be hashed into a number). Leave blank for a random seed.
                • Click 🎲 Roll to generate a random seed you can share with friends to play the same map.
                • The seed is saved with the game state, so continuing a game always restores the same map.

                Terrain Density

                • Four sliders control how much of each terrain type the map generator places: Forest, Water (lakes & rivers), City, and Village.
                • Each slider ranges from 0× (none) to 3.0× (triple density). Default is 1.0×.
                • Forest 0×: open battlefield with no cover. Forest 3×: dense woodland with heavy concealment.
                • Water 0×: no lakes or rivers. Water 3×: many waterways creating chokepoints.
                • City 0×: minimal settlements (at least 1 per player guaranteed). City 3×: urban-heavy maps with more income sources.
                • Village 0×: no villages. Village 3×: dense villages clustered around cities.
                • Terrain weights are saved with the game, so the map regenerates identically when you continue.

                Diplomacy & alliances

                • Games with 3–8 countries include a full diplomacy system. Access it from the 🤝 Diplomacy button.
                • Each pair of countries is in one of three states: War (default), Peace, or Alliance.
                • War: units attack on sight; territory is contested.
                • Peace: units cannot attack each other, but cannot pass through each other's territory either.
                • Alliance: units can move through allied territory and stacks. Allies can send each other gold.
                • AI Takeover: when handing your turn to the AI, choose a strategy — Focus Attack (aggressive), Focus Defend (cautious), or Balanced (default).
                • Elimination: a country is eliminated when it has no units for 3 turns or no cities for 7 turns. HQ destruction is instant (see HQ section below).
                • PvE: if the human player is eliminated, the game ends immediately — AI countries fighting each other won't continue without you.
                • Peace deals: eliminated territory is redistributed among the countries that fought it, proportional to damage dealt. Cities count for 2 points, villages for 1.
                • Victory: the game ends when only one country (or alliance bloc) survives.
